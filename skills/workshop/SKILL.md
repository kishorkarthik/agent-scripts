# Workshop

Use this skill to access and maintain Karthik's personal context. Consult it when a task requires understanding his long-term direction, current priorities, ongoing commitments, personal history, or accumulated progress.

## Sources

- Strategy (read first, always): `~/projects/workshop/VISION.md` — overarching direction and long-term aspirations.
- Repository: `~/projects/workshop`
- Planning: `~/projects/workshop/plan/` — annual plans, 5 year outlook, and quaterly plans.
- Annual plan: `~/projects/workshop/plan/Annual/`
- 5 Year outlook: `~/projects/workshop/plan/Outlook/`
- Journal: `~/projects/workshop/Journal/` — reflections, learning, and personal insights.
- logs: `~/projects/workshop/log/` — chronological records of activities and progress.

## Scope

Use Workshop for:

- Understanding personal goals, priorities, values, and long-term direction.
- Reviewing current plans and commitments.
- Tracking progress and identifying gaps or recurring patterns.
- Recalling previous decisions, reflections, and lessons.
- Evaluating opportunities and decisions against established priorities.
- Updating plans, journal entries, and progress logs when explicitly requested.

Project-specific code, implementation details, and technical documentation belong in their respective repositories. Workshop provides the broader personal context for that work.

## Context Retrieval

1. Read `VISION.md` first to understand the overarching direction.
2. Identify the relevant planning documents before making recommendations about goals or priorities.
3. Consult recent progress logs when assessing current status.
4. Consult journal entries when historical reasoning, personal reflections, or lessons are relevant.
5. Prefer the latest applicable decisions and plans over outdated historical records.
6. Read selectively. Do not scan the entire repository without a reason.

## Interpretation

- Treat Workshop as the primary source of truth for recorded personal objectives, plans, decisions, and progress.
- Distinguish established commitments from ideas, aspirations, and possibilities.
- Do not assume an old plan is still active simply because it exists.
- Consider historical context, but do not let outdated decisions override current priorities.
- Identify conflicts between stated goals and current actions when relevant. Do not silently reconcile contradictions.
- Never invent missing progress, decisions, or commitments. Ask when an important ambiguity cannot be resolved from the records.

## Updates

Workshop is read-only by default.

When explicitly asked to make changes:

1. Identify the appropriate existing document.
2. Read its surrounding context and follow its established structure and conventions.
3. Make the smallest necessary changes.
4. Preserve historical records; do not rewrite past entries to reflect present understanding.
5. Review the resulting diff for unintended changes.
6. Do not commit, push, or reorganize the repository unless explicitly requested.

## Safety

- Never read any other sensitive files as part of routine context gathering.
- Do not expose sensitive personal information unnecessarily.
- Do not modify or delete personal records without explicit authorization.
